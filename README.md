
# PelviAI
### AI-Assisted Analysis of Pelvic Floor Mobility and Deformation

PelviAI is a research project exploring the application of artificial intelligence and computational image analysis to transperineal ultrasound imaging.

The project focuses on characterizing the spatial and temporal behavior of pelvic structures during dynamic ultrasound examinations, with particular interest in mobility, deformation, and movement patterns.

## Research Objectives

- Investigate pelvic structure mobility using ultrasound image sequences.
- Explore spatial and temporal changes in anatomical structures.
- Develop computational approaches for analyzing movement and deformation.
- Investigate methods for identifying relevant phases of dynamic examinations.
- Explore quantitative biomarkers derived from ultrasound imaging.

## Research Approach

The proposed workflow includes:

1. Dataset exploration and quality assessment.
2. Ultrasound image and annotation processing.
3. Extraction of geometric and temporal features.
4. Characterization of pelvic structure mobility and deformation.
5. Investigation of dynamic examination phases.
6. Development and evaluation of computational models.
7. Visualization and interpretation of results.

## Technologies

The project uses Python-based computational workflows, including Google Colab and Jupyter notebooks.

Additional libraries, algorithms, and analytical methods will be documented as the research progresses.

## Project Status

**Status:** Exploratory research and methodological development.

The computational workflow and analytical methods are under development. Research objectives and implementation details may be refined as the dataset is explored.

## Research Applications

PelviAI aims to contribute to the development of quantitative and reproducible approaches for investigating pelvic floor dynamics using transperineal ultrasound.
